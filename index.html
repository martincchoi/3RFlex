Middle School Friday 3R Flex Circles

Explore options, find your interest, and discover what’s available for each quarter.

📌 Important Rules & Guidelines

  • Default Placement: 3R Study Hall for relearn/reassessment is the default placement if choices aren't submitted or when not in a circle.
  • Academic Priority: Academic relearning/reassessments always take precedence over Flex Circles upon teacher request.
  • Commitment: Choose only if you intend to actively participate. Circle leaders may request removals for disrespect or distraction.
  • Switching Policy: No switching between circles once assigned. Drops allowed only after Session 1 via email to Mr. Choi with a valid reason.
🎨 Creative Arts

The Calm Creative Classroom

Open to All Capacity: 5–10

Need a break from the busy school day? This Flex class is your chance to relax, recharge, and get creative! You can enjoy activities like watercolor painting, card making, and other creative projects. No artistic skills are needed—just bring your creativity.

Leader: Erika New
🎨 Creative Arts

Three-Panel Comics

Open to All Capacity: 10–15

Students will learn the form and theory behind the three-panel comic form. We will then work on drafting, editing, illustrating, and publishing a collection of our work.

Leader: Timothy Stewart
🎨 Creative Arts

Photography Design and Storytelling

Open to All Capacity: Flexible

Leader: John (Phillip) Shrier
🎵 Music & Performing

Flute

Open to All Capacity: 5–10

We will work on the foundations of flute playing like tone, breath control, tonguing, fingerings. We will work our way through the major and minor scales; learn some basic music theory; and have fun playing solos, duets, and flute choir arrangements together. (Note: Instrument required)

Leader: Julie Flaming
🎵 Music & Performing

Chapel Vocals

Open to All (No 6th until S2) Capacity: 5–10

This Flex Circle is for ALL students who plan on singing in chapel band this quarter. This is not required for instrumentalists. Students will meet in Mrs. Ernst's room to prepare vocally for the following week's group rehearsal.

Leaders: Rachel Ernst & Jessica Ridgely
